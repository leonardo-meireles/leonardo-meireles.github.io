Atlas Jiu-Jitsu

open source live

A knowledge graph for Brazilian jiu-jitsu. Every position is a node; every sweep, submission, and guard pass is a directed edge — so you see the whole game at once instead of one technique video at a time. I wrote a 400-line parser for GrappleMap's base62 skeletal format, then a custom Three.js renderer doing quaternion SLERP between joints — the figures are geometry, not images. 11 structural validators run at test time to catch curation errors before they ship.
